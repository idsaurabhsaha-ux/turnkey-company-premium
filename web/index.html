A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

What does a turnkey interior project include?

Design coordination, material planning, procurement and execution against an agreed project scope. Kitchens, wardrobes, furniture and finishing works are specified together in your proposal.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Essential, Elevate and Premium are starting specifications. The final materials, quantities, room scope and upgrades are confirmed for your space in the approved proposal.

Can I enquire for a project outside Chhattisgarh?

Yes. Share your city and project brief. The Turnkey Company welcomes enquiries across India; execution feasibility and timelines are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

Is the calculator a final quotation?

No. This prototype uses illustrative rates to demonstrate the experience. It shows a planning range with a separate GST assumption. Actual pricing requires verified measurements, final selections and a project-specific quotation.
